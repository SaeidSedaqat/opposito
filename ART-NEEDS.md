# Opposito: art needs

Right now every visual is drawn in code (SVG pieces, CSS UI). Nothing below blocks the game. This is what would make it look finished.

## 1. Piece art (highest impact)
9 skins × 2 pieces = **18 piece illustrations**. Square, transparent PNG or SVG, 256×256 minimum, readable at 32px. The two pieces in a pair must be easy to tell apart by shape as well as color.

| Skin | Piece A | Piece B | Unlock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire & Ice | Fire | Ice | default |
| Cat & Dog | Cat | Dog | login day 2 |
| Sun & Moon | Sun | Moon | ad |
| Pizza & Burger | Pizza | Burger | 500 coins |
| Ninja & Pirate | Ninja | Pirate | login day 6 |
| Robot & Alien | Robot | Alien | 650 coins |
| Cactus & Flower | Cactus | Flower | 400 coins |
| Coffee & Tea | Coffee | Tea | 300 coins |
| Sword & Shield | Sword | Shield | 800 coins |

Nice to have: a 2–4 frame "pop" or idle wiggle per piece.

## 2. Branding (needed for store/portal submission)
- **App icon / favicon**: 512×512 and 192×192 PNG, plus 32×32 favicon. *Currently missing.*
- **Logo / wordmark** "Opposito" for the Home and loading screens.
- **Portal cover images**: 1920×1080 landscape, 800×1200 portrait, 512×512 square (Playgama/CrazyGames/Poki sizes).
- **Social preview** (og:image) 1200×630.
- 3–5 **store screenshots** (can be captured from the game once art is in).

## 3. Board and effects
- Board background texture or tile pattern (one per skin, or one neutral).
- Frozen (given) cell texture, subtle, so it reads as locked.
- Link "melt" connector graphic (the mark between two linked cells).
- Win particle sprites (sparks, confetti), ideally themed per skin.
- Mistake highlight (soft red glow) and hint highlight.

## 4. UI icon set (15 icons, same style)
ad, back, bag (shop), check, coin, gear (settings), help, hint, home, lock, pause, play, reveal, tick, undo.
SVG, single-color or two-tone, 24px grid.

## 5. Shop and rewards (optional)
- Skin card backgrounds / frames for the shop.
- Booster icons, larger versions for reward popups (Hint, Check, Reveal).
- Daily reward / calendar illustration, coin pile, chest.
- Mode tiles on Home: Levels, Daily, Free Play.
